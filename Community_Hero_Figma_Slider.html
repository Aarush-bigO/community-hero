01 / PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Urban Infrastructure Crisis

Why traditional municipal grievance mechanisms fail Indian cities
COMMUNITY HERO • IIIT BHOPAL
GROUND REALITY: UNRESOLVED CIVIC DEFECTS
Severe potholes, collapsed drains, and dark streetlights cause over 3,500 preventable road accidents annually across Indian metropolitan roads.
1. Form Friction & Citizen Abandonment
Existing municipal portals require multi-field forms, mandatory desktop logins, and lack visual feedback. Over 68% of citizens abandon complaint submission mid-way.
2. 42% Duplicate Dispatch Overhead
When an arterial pothole opens, 12–15 nearby residents file independent complaints. Municipalities dispatch duplicate inspection teams, wasting ~₹42,000/month per ward in fuel and crew hours.
3. The 'Black Hole' Citizen Disengagement
Reports remain in 'Pending' status indefinitely without live status feeds or geolocation pins. Lack of civic transparency leads to voter apathy and decaying infrastructure.

05 / SPATIAL DEDUPLICATION

75-Meter Geospatial Deduplication Engine

Eliminating redundant municipal truck dispatches using spherical distance clustering
COMMUNITY HERO • IIIT BHOPAL
HAVERSINE DISTANCE CLUSTERING
Geodesic Surface Distance Computation
d = 2R · arcsin(√(sin²(Δlat/2) + cos(lat1)·cos(lat2)·sin²(Δlng/2)))
where R = 6,371,000 meters (Mean Earth Radius)
  • 1. Incoming Ingestion: Coordinate (lat, lng) received with ML category C.
  • 2. Spatial Query: Database queries active tickets within a bounding box (±0.001°) matching category C.
  • 3. Precise Haversine Check: Computes geodesic distance d to each candidate.
  • 4. Automated Clustering: If d ≤ 75 meters:
    • Mark ticket as: duplicate_of = parent_id
    • Increment parent: verification_count += 1
    • Block redundant work order dispatch.
FIELD VALIDATION & MUNICIPAL ROI
Distance Boundary Case Studies
  • Case Study 1 (Inside Boundary — 38 meters):
    Citizen A reports pothole at (23.2599, 77.4126) → Ticket #101 created.
    Citizen B reports same pothole 38m away → Merged into #101.

  • Case Study 2 (Outside Boundary — 98 meters):

THREE.JS WEBGL 3D DIGITAL TWIN
Spherical-to-Cartesian Coordinate Transformation
X = -R · sin(φ) · cos(θ)
Y = R · cos(φ)
Z = R · sin(φ) · sin(θ)
where φ = (90 - lat) · π/180, θ = (lng + 180) · π/180
  • Dynamic Beacon Pulse Shaders: Custom GLSL fragment shaders animate concentric wave rings around pins, with expansion frequency proportional to ticket urgency (1 to 5).
  • Raycasting Hit-Testing: GPU ray-sphere intersections detect cursor hover over incident coordinates with sub-millisecond raycast response.
  • 60 FPS Frame Budget: Optimized geometry instancing maintains smooth 60 FPS on integrated graphics hardware without thermal throttling.
