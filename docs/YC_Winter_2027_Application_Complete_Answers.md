### What is your company going to make? Please describe your product and what it does or will do.

> Every city on Earth today is completely blind and deaf. You can summon a rideshare car in 3 minutes, but if a commercial power transformer explodes or an underground water main ruptures, modern cities still run on 1990s telephone switchboards and 15-minute IVR phone trees. Citizens abandon forms, 911 lines get jammed, and because 50 callers describe the same incident differently, cities send multiple repair trucks to the same spot, wasting 40% of their operational fleet budget.
> 
> **Community Hero is the autonomous AI nervous system for city emergency dispatch and infrastructure maintenance.**
> 
> Our platform integrates three closed-loop multimodal layers:
> 
> 1. **Zero-Friction Inbound Triage (Voice & Photo):** Citizens dial in or tap a mobile button and speak naturally in any language (English, Spanish, Hindi, etc.). In under 850 milliseconds, our Voice AI talks back with natural human empathy, extracts exact coordinates, rates urgency from 1 to 10, and generates an Open311-compliant Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) ticket. Citizens can also snap 1-click photos with automatic reverse-geocoding.
> 
> 2. **Passive Fleet Edge AI (The Moving City Scanner):** We mount smart edge-AI camera modules on existing municipal transit fleets (public buses, garbage trucks, and municipal vans). As these vehicles drive daily routes, onboard computer vision passively scans roads 24/7—auto-detecting potholes, street flooding, fallen powerlines, and illegal dumping before humans even call them in.
> 
> 3. **The Central Brain (75-Meter Spatial Swarm Clustering):** When 40 people call about the same crisis, our spatial clustering engine merges every report within a 75-meter walking radius into one master ticket in under 4ms. It upgrades priority, prevents redundant crew dispatches, and reassures callers that help is already en route.
> 
> 4. **Verified Closed-Loop Field Execution & 3D Digital Twin:** Dispatched municipal workers receive GPS work orders and must upload a computer-vision-verified "AFTER" photo to resolve tickets—ending fake government completions. City leaders monitor real-time incident beacons on an interactive 3D digital twin command center.

### Idea

#### Why did you pick this idea to work on? Do you have domain expertise in this area? How do you know people need what you’re making?
> We built this because we lived through the failure firsthand. A few months ago, a commercial transformer exploded on a busy street near us, showering sparks on pedestrians. People were screaming, pulling out phones, trying to call hotlines—only to be met with busy signals or 15-minute wait times. One bystander tried the official municipal grievance app, saw a wall of English text asking for "Ward Number" and "Sub-district ID", swore, and gave up. Three separate fire trucks arrived 45 minutes later because dispatchers logged three separate calls.
> 
> That experience made us realize that while we have autonomous cars and 10-minute grocery delivery, our physical cities are still operating on 1990s telephone switchboards and dead web portals.
> 
> We spoke to 911 dispatchers, municipal call center operators, and residents across multiple cities. Every single dispatcher echoed the same pain point: *"During a storm, 40% to 50% of our calls are duplicates from people standing on the exact same street corner, and our software can't tell them apart."* When we built our sub-850ms voice triage and 75-meter spatial clustering, dispatchers immediately recognized that this solves their single biggest operational bottleneck.

#### Who are your competitors? What do you understand about your business that they don’t?
> **Competitors:**
> 1. **Legacy CAD Giants (Motorola Solutions, CentralSquare, Hexagon):** They control 90% of the 911/CAD market with 30-year-old on-premise software. They charge millions, move at the speed of government lobbying, and have zero real-time voice AI or automated spatial deduplication.
> 2. **Citizen Form Apps (SeeClickFix / CivicPlus):** They rely on citizens manually typing forms and picking dropdown categories. They fail because people do not type during emergencies (>70% drop-off rate).
> 
> **What we understand that they don’t:**
> - **Emergency reporting must be zero-typing:** In a crisis, humans only speak or snap a photo. If your software requires keyboard input, you have already lost.
> - **Cities don't lack trucks; they suffer from an information compression bottleneck:** Dispatchers waste up to 40% of fleet fuel sending multiple crews to the same emergency because callers describe the same spot using different landmarks. Spatial deduplication at the point of ingestion is the highest-ROI software feature a city can buy.
> - **Drop-in Open311 adoption beats multi-year RFP cycles:** We don't ask cities to replace their core database. We plug in as an intelligent AI voice & vision layer on top of their existing CAD systems via standard Open311 APIs in 48 hours.
